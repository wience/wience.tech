Winzen's Talk Machine privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Talk Machine collects no data. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPad.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • It sends nothing to me or to anyone else. The app makes no network requests on its own.

What stays on your iPad

  • Your boards, words and settings.
  • Photos you take or choose for a button, and voice recordings you make for a button.
  • The usage log, if you turn it on. It is off by default, it records which words were tapped and when, and you can export or clear it in Settings.
  • Backups of your boards.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. If iCloud Backup is on for your iPad, Apple includes app data in your device backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

Permissions

Talk Machine asks for the camera only when you take a photo for a button, and for the microphone only when you record a voice for a button. Choosing from your photos uses Apple's photo picker, so the app sees only the picture you pick. Pictures and recordings are saved inside the app and never leave the iPad unless you export a backup yourself.

Website buttons

You can set up a button that opens a website. Tapping it opens that site in the browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies. The app has none of these buttons until someone adds one.

Files you share

When you export a backup or a usage log, you choose where the file goes. Talk Machine doesn't keep a copy anywhere else.

Children

Talk Machine is suitable for children and collects no information from them or from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
